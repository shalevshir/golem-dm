# SRD Content

Only content from the D&D System Reference Document 5.2.1 may be placed here.
ADR-0001 fixed the edition at 2024 / SRD 5.2.1, so SRD 5.1 (2014) material is
out of scope as well.

SRD 5.2.1 is licensed CC-BY-4.0 and **requires attribution**. The exact required
statement, and the rules for contributors, are in [`NOTICE.md`](../../NOTICE.md)
at the repository root. Reproduce it verbatim; do not add any other attribution
to Wizards of the Coast.

No non-SRD monsters (beholder, mind flayer...), named settings, or non-SRD
subclasses — those are not covered by the license.

Files are JSON validated against @ai-dm/schemas at load time.
POC scope: ~10 monsters, 15 conditions/spells, 4 classes.
